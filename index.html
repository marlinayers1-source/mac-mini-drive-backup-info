Personal backup utility

Mac mini Drive Backup

AboutPrivacy policyUse information

A private, single-user utility that saves versioned copies of the owner’s Google Drive files to storage attached to the owner’s Mac mini.

Read-only Google Drive access. The utility can list and download files. It cannot change or delete the files in Google Drive.

What it saves

Ordinary files, available exports of Google Docs, Sheets and Slides, and file information needed to identify and recover those copies. Older local copies are retained when source files change.

Where it runs

The backup software runs locally on the owner’s Mac mini using rclone and a local backup routine. Downloaded files stay in the owner’s local backup storage. This website is an information page, not a file-upload service.

Who can use it

This utility is for its owner’s personal use. It does not offer accounts or a backup service to the public.
