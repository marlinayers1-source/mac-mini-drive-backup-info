This policy describes the owner’s personal Mac mini Drive Backup utility.

Google data accessed

With the owner’s consent, the utility requests read-only Google Drive access. It uses file contents and metadata, including names, identifiers, folder relationships, modification times and available checksums, to make and verify local backups. Google-native documents are exported to downloadable formats where supported.

Use and storage

Google data is used only to create, verify and recover the owner’s backups. Copies and backup logs are stored on the owner’s Mac mini and attached local storage. Authorization credentials are stored locally with access limited to the owner’s computer account.

Sharing

The utility does not sell Google data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. It does not upload the downloaded Drive contents to this website or to a separate hosted backup service. It communicates with Google to authorize access and download the requested data.

Retention and control

Local versions remain until the owner removes them. Removing a file from Google Drive does not automatically remove older backup copies. The owner can stop the local schedule or revoke the utility’s access through Google Account connections. Revoking access stops future downloads but does not erase existing local copies.

This information website

This static website has no sign-in, upload form, advertising or analytics added by the utility. Its hosting provider may process ordinary request information to deliver the site. No Drive files or authorization credentials are published here.

Contact

This is a personal utility, not a public service. The owner can use the developer support address shown on its Google consent screen for questions about the configuration.

The utility’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
