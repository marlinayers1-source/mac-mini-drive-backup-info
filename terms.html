Mac mini Drive Backup is a personal utility for its owner. It is not offered as a hosted service and does not accept other users’ files.

Backup operation depends on the Mac mini, attached storage, available space, internet access and valid Google authorization. The owner should review backup results and test recovery. Google exports may not reproduce every original Google Workspace feature or revision.

Backups retain older copies. The owner controls their storage, retention and removal, and should keep recovery passwords separately.

This information page creates no paid subscription or service commitment.
